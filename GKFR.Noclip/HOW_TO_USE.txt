# Noclip Mod

## Description
Fly the local kart through walls and floors. F8 toggles, F11 first-person. WASD move, Space/Ctrl up/down, Shift faster, mouse rotates (pitch+yaw).

## Installation
1. Copy GKFR.Noclip.dll to your BepInEx/plugins folder
2. Launch the game

## How to Use
1. Press F8 to toggle noclip on/off
2. While active:
   - WASD: Fly forward/back and strafe left/right
   - Space/Ctrl: Fly up/down (world directions)
   - Shift: Hold to fly faster
   - Mouse: Rotate (pitch + yaw)
3. Press F11 to toggle first-person camera

## Configuration
Edit BepInEx/config/com.gkfr.noclip.cfg to adjust settings:

- General.Enabled: Enable/disable the mod (default: true)
- General.ToggleKey: Toggle key (default: F8)
- General.FirstPersonKey: First-person toggle (default: F11)
- Movement.MoveSpeed: Fly speed (default: 25)
- Movement.FastMoveSpeed: Fast speed (default: 90)
- Movement.LookSensitivity: Mouse sensitivity (default: 3)
- Movement.StrafeWithArrows: Arrow keys strafe (default: true)
- Movement.InvertLookY: Invert mouse Y (default: false)
- FirstPerson.Height: Camera height (default: 0.8)
- FirstPerson.Forward: Camera forward offset (default: 0.3)

## Keybindings
- F8: Toggle noclip on/off
- F11: Toggle first-person camera
- WASD: Movement
- Space/Ctrl: Up/Down
- Shift: Fast movement
- Mouse: Rotation

## Notes
- The kart passes through walls and floors
- Physics are suspended while noclip is active
- First-person view parks camera on the kart
- HUD wrong-way indicator is suppressed
