Godmode Mod
===========

Makes karts invincible to all damage and collisions.

Controls:
- F5 - Toggle godmode on/off (cycles through modes)

Modes (cycle through with each F5 press):
0. Player only - Only your kart is invincible
1. All humans - All human player karts are invincible  
2. All karts - All karts (human and AI) are invincible
3. AI only - Only AI karts are invincible

Configuration:
- Enabled (default: true) - Master switch
- ToggleKey (default: F5) - Key to toggle godmode
- DisableDamage (default: true) - Disable damage to karts when godmode is active
- GodmodeMode (default: 0) - Mode selection (0-3 as listed above)

This mod affects karts based on the selected mode. The default mode (0) makes only your kart invincible.
