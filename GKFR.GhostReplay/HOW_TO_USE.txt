# Ghost Replay Mod

## Description
Record your best lap, replay it as a translucent kart you race against.

## Installation
1. Copy GKFR.GhostReplay.dll to your BepInEx/plugins folder
2. Launch the game

## How to Use
1. Press F10 to toggle ghost replay on/off
2. Press F11 to start/stop recording your lap
3. Press F2 to play back the ghost replay
4. Adjust ghost transparency in settings (0-1 range)

## Configuration
Edit BepInEx/config/com.gkfr.ghostreplay.cfg to adjust settings:

- General.Enabled: Enable/disable the mod (default: true)
- General.ToggleKey: Toggle ghost replay on/off (default: F10)
- Recording.RecordKey: Key to start/stop recording (default: F11)
- Recording.ReplayKey: Key to play back ghost replay (default: F2)
- Display.GhostTransparency: Transparency level of ghost kart (default: 0.5)

## Keybindings
- F10: Toggle ghost replay on/off
- F11: Start/stop recording
- F2: Play back ghost replay

## Notes
- This mod is useful for time-trial practice
- The ghost is translucent so you can see it through walls
- Implementation requires recording and replaying kart positions/movements
