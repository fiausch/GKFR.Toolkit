Ghost / Time-Trial Replay Mod
=============================

Record your best lap, replay it as a translucent kart you race against.

Controls:
- F10  toggle ghost replay on/off
- F11  start/stop recording
- F2   play back ghost replay

Configuration:
- Enabled (default: true) - Master switch
- ToggleKey (default: F10) - Key to toggle ghost replay
- RecordKey (default: F11) - Key to start/stop recording
- ReplayKey (default: F2) - Key to play back ghost replay
- GhostTransparency (default: 0.5) - Transparency level of ghost kart (0-1)

Records your best lap and replays it as a translucent kart for comparison.
