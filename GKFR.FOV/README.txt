FOV Mod
===========

Wider FOV and pushed far-clip for a less claustrophobic view.

Controls:
- F12 - Toggle FOV adjustment on/off

Configuration:
- Enabled (default: true) - Master switch
- ToggleKey (default: F12) - Key to toggle FOV adjustment
- FovMultiplier (default: 1.5) - FOV multiplier 
- FarClipMultiplier (default: 3.0) - Far clip multiplier

Adjusts the camera's field of view and far clipping plane to provide a wider, less claustrophobic view.
