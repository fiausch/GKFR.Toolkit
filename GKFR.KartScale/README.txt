Kart Scale Mod
==============

Grow/shrink your kart + hitbox (the spectator mod already scales kart objects).

Controls:
- NUMPAD +  increase kart scale
- NUMPAD -  decrease kart scale

Configuration:
- Enabled (default: true) - Master switch
- ScaleUpKey (default: NUMPAD +) - Key to increase kart scale
- ScaleDownKey (default: NUMPAD -) - Key to decrease kart scale
- ScaleStep (default: 0.1) - Amount to change scale per key press
- MinScale (default: 0.5) - Minimum scale allowed
- MaxScale (default: 2.0) - Maximum scale allowed

Adjusts the size of your kart and hitbox for different racing experiences.
